01 / Overview

About the laboratory

This demonstration page presents a sample overview of a software laboratory supporting practical work in electronics, communication, programming, and simulation.

Learning through practical work

Software tools help students model systems, write and test programs, simulate circuits, explore communication networks, and connect classroom concepts with engineering applications. The exact activities depend on the subject, experiment, and facilities available.
